This document catalogs all deterministic security findings generated by the **TrustLens Certificate Analyzer Rule Engine**. Each finding includes its canonical code, default severity level, trust score deduction, title, and actionable remediation advice.

## 2. Severity Classification Summary

- **CRITICAL** (Deductions: 80 - 100): Indicates immediate security failure, structural invalidity, or active vulnerability. Unconditionally caps trust score at <= 40, rendering verdict `DISTRUSTED`.
- **HIGH** (Deductions: 40 - 70): Represents high-risk operational defects, missing intermediate chains, weak keys, or urgent pending expiration. Caps score and prevents `TRUSTED` verdict.
- **MEDIUM** (Deductions: 20 - 25): Represents standard non-compliance (e.g. CA/B Forum validity duration exceeded, missing SANs, or expiring in < 30 days). Results in `WARNING` verdict if no criticals exist.
- **LOW** (Deductions: 5 - 10): Minor informational degradation (e.g. unverified revocation freshness).
- **INFO** (Deductions: 0): Successful verifications or contextual operational status notes.
